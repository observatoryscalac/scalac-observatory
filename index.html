About the data

Indicators collected from SCALAC HPC centers for 2025. Usage figures are core-hours reported by each site’s accounting system (typically Slurm sreport).

Reported
Total core-hours in the period (≈ Allocated + Down + Planned down + Idle + Planned).
Allocated
Core-hours allocated to user jobs.
Down / Planned down
Core-hours lost to unplanned outages / scheduled maintenance.
Planned
Slurm “Planned”: core-hours during which eligible jobs were waiting in the queue but could not start (size or time limits). It reflects queued demand, not maintenance.
Idle
Core-hours available but not used.
Utilization
% use over available = Allocated ÷ (Reported − Down − Planned down).
Downtime
% down over total = (Down + Planned down) ÷ Reported.
Rpeak CPU FP64
Theoretical peak double-precision performance of the CPUs, in TFlop/s.
Rpeak GPU FP16
Theoretical peak half-precision performance of the accelerators, in TFlop/s (relevant for AI workloads). Not comparable with FP64.
Personnel
Staff operating the system: full-time / half-time / simple dedication.
Power & PUE
Data-center maximum power (kW), Power Usage Effectiveness and measured cluster peak consumption (kW).
Location
Approximate, at city level, based on the institution’s main campus.
Gaps
Some centers could not provide accounting data; their usage indicators are shown as “n/a”.
